The loop

  1. Tap a room to throw the glass ball. It shatters against the back wall and the ants drop to the nearest floor.
  2. Ants crawl along floors, walls, ceilings and furniture. A swarm eats any food whose number is at or below its size.
  3. Every bite flies to the colony, which hatches new ants into the next ball.
  4. Ants only live a short while once they land. The bar under a swarm's count shows how long it has left.
  5. Eat the red objectives before the clock runs out.
